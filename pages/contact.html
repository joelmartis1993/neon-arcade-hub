Frequently Asked Questions

❓ Are all games on Neon Rush Games free to play?

Yes! All our free browser-based arcade games are completely free with no hidden costs, subscriptions, or pay-to-win mechanics. Play instantly without any barriers or registration requirements.

❓ Do I need to create an account to play?

No account is required! Play all games immediately without any registration. Your scores and progress are stored locally on your device, ensuring your privacy while maintaining your achievement history.

❓ How do I report a bug?

If you encounter any technical issues or bugs, please use our contact form above or email us directly at officialmail316@gmail.com with the subject line "Bug Report". Include details about the issue and the game affected. We take all bug reports seriously and work to fix issues promptly.

❓ Can I suggest new games or features?

Absolutely! We love hearing suggestions from our community. Use the contact form to share your game ideas, feature requests, or gameplay feedback. Your ideas help shape the future of Neon Rush Games and ensure we're building the games players want to enjoy.

❓ How quickly do you respond to emails?

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24-48 hours. Response times may vary depending on volume, but we prioritize all messages and make every effort to address your concerns promptly. If you don't receive a response within two business days, please follow up with another message.

❓ Is there an alternative way to reach you besides email?

Currently, email and our online contact form are the primary ways to reach us. Both methods go directly to our team. Check our About page for more information about Neon Rush Games and our commitment to player support.
