Neon Survivor — Free Vampire Survivors-Style Arcade Game

Survive endless waves of neon enemies, auto-attack your way through hordes, level up, and choose powerful upgrades — all in your browser, no download required.

About Neon Survivor

Neon Survivor drops you into a pulsating cyberpunk arena where survival is the only objective. Inspired by the addictive loop of Vampire Survivors, this free browser survival game strips away complexity and replaces it with pure arcade adrenaline. Your character attacks automatically — your job is to move, dodge, and decide which upgrades will keep you alive longest. Every few seconds a new wave crashes in, each denser and faster than the last, and the XP orbs left behind by fallen enemies fuel a leveling system that lets you shape your build on the fly.

With sixteen distinct upgrades ranging from orbital shields and piercing projectiles to area-of-effect novas and health regeneration, no two runs play out the same way. Six enemy archetypes — from swarm drones and charging brutes to teleporting phantoms and screen-filling bosses — force you to constantly adapt your strategy. The roguelite progression keeps you hooked: whether you're chasing a personal best in Endless mode, battling back-to-back bosses in Boss Rush, surviving with a single hit point in Hardcore, or competing on the seeded Daily leaderboard, Neon Survivor offers hundreds of hours of replayability with zero installation.

Built entirely with HTML5 Canvas and JavaScript, the game delivers smooth 60fps wave survival action directly in your browser with no plugins or downloads. The Web Audio engine generates a reactive cyberpunk soundtrack that intensifies as enemy density ramps up — bass pulses quicken, synth layers stack, and percussion shifts to match the chaos on screen. A minimap in the corner tracks enemy positions across the full arena, and screen shake on every boss kill and critical hit delivers the kind of visceral feedback that keeps your palms sweating. Whether you're playing on a desktop during a break or on your phone during a commute, the responsive canvas scaling and virtual joystick make every session feel tight and precise.

How to Play

Move your character to dodge incoming enemies — combat is fully automatic. As you defeat enemies, collect the XP orbs they drop to fill your experience bar. Each level-up presents three random upgrade cards; pick the one that best complements your current build. Survive as long as possible, rack up kills, and push your high score higher every run. Boss enemies appear at regular intervals and require quick reflexes and smart positioning to defeat.
