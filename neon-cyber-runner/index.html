About Neon Cyber Runner

Neon Cyber Runner drops you into the heart of a sprawling cyberpunk metropolis rendered entirely in glowing neon wireframes. As a rogue data courier sprinting across the rooftops of a dystopian megacity, your mission is simple: run as far as you can without being caught. The city throws everything it has at you — towering energy barriers that crackle with pink lightning, razor-thin laser gates that sweep across your path, and shifting platforms that collapse the moment you touch them. Every second you survive, the world accelerates, the music intensifies, and the obstacles stack up faster and meaner than before.

Scattered across the neon grid are glowing power cells — collectible energy orbs that boost your score multiplier and charge your shield meter. Grab enough of them and you'll activate a temporary electromagnetic shield that lets you smash through a single barrier unharmed. Timing your jumps, slides, and power cell pickups becomes a high-stakes rhythm game as you chase the next high score. The procedurally generated obstacle patterns mean no two runs are ever identical, keeping the gameplay fresh across hundreds of attempts.

The retro-arcade aesthetic is powered by a custom canvas renderer that delivers silky-smooth 60fps animation alongside a Web Audio synthesized soundtrack that reacts dynamically to your speed. Whether you're on a desktop with a mechanical keyboard or on a phone during your commute, the controls feel tight and responsive. Jump into the neon grid and see how long you can survive.

How to Play

Press Play to start your run. Your character sprints forward automatically — your job is to react to incoming obstacles. Jump over low barriers and energy fences by pressing Space or swiping up on mobile. Slide under laser gates and overhead hazards by pressing the Down arrow or swiping down. Collect the floating power cells to increase your score multiplier. The longer you run, the faster the world moves. One collision and it's game over — but you can always retry instantly and try to beat your personal best.
